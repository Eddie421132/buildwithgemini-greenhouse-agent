# My agent: greenhouse-agent
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant enthusiasts and gardeners care for their plants with a catalog of indoor & outdoor plants and care schedules.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User's plant care experience, indoor lighting condition, and watering preferences
- Tools: Look up plant catalog, get plant care tips, add new plants, update plant care logs
- Catalog/UI: Collection of greenhouse plants (name, species, light, water, care_level, description)
- Image gen: Generate visuals of thriving plants in home settings
- Sandbox: Calculate watering schedules based on temperature and pot size

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Firestore database storage, A2UI cards
